# Greenhouse

Up: [[Home]]

The idea incubator. Every marketing spark, design concept, venture thought,
and out-of-the-box notion gets one page in `wiki/ideas/` and moves through a
fixed lifecycle, so ideas mature over time instead of getting lost.

> [!idea] The lifecycle
> **seedling** (raw capture, rough is fine) → **budding** (being developed:
> evidence, experiments, sketches) → **evergreen** (mature — ready to act on,
> usually by becoming a decision page) · **parked** (deliberately
> shelved, with the reason noted). The `tended` date is updated every time an
> idea is meaningfully worked on.

## How the Greenhouse is kept

- **Plant**: tell the agent "Plant an idea: …" — it creates the page from
  [[Idea Template]] with `status: seedling` and today's `planted` date.
- **Tend**: any session that develops an idea updates its `tended` date and,
  when earned, promotes its status.
- **Resurface**: the weekly review lists seedlings untouched for 30+ days —
  each gets tended, promoted, or honestly parked. Nothing silently rots.
- **Graduate**: an evergreen idea the business commits to becomes a decision
  page in `wiki/decisions/`, and the idea page links to it.
